Ember

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ember is a habit-tracking app made by Emberglow. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: Ember keeps your data on your own device and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Ember has no accounts and no servers. There is nothing to sign up for. Your habits, streaks, check-offs, photos, and settings live only in the app's private storage on your phone.

What data the app stores (on your device)

  • Your habits and progress — habit names, schedules, completion history, streaks, and streak-freeze records.
  • Proof photos — if you use photo proof, the photo you take is saved on your device and shown in the app. It is never uploaded.
  • App settings — such as your chosen reminder times and coach tone.

All of the above is stored locally. The developer cannot see it and never receives a copy.

Device permissions

  • Camera — used only when you choose to take a photo as proof of completing a habit. Photos stay on your device.
  • Notifications — used to show the habit reminders you schedule. These are generated on your device.

Backups

If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Android may back up the app's data to your own Google Drive so it can be restored if you reinstall. This backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account and settings — not by the developer, who has no access to it. You can disable it in your device's backup settings.

What Ember does NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, or identifiers.

Children

Ember is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
